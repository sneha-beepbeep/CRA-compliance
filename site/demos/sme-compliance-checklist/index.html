EU AI Act Website Checklist

For small business owners whose website has AI-generated content. This walkthrough uses a fictional bakery; nothing you answer leaves your browser.

The compliance problem

More and more small businesses use AI to write blog posts, make product images or answer customers in a chat window. Article 50 of the EU AI Act sets transparency rules for some of this: people should know when they are talking to an AI, and some AI-generated content has to be disclosed.

Most owners aren't lawyers and don't have time to read the regulation. They want a straight answer to one question: is there anything on my website I need to look at?

The checklist asks a few short factual questions per item, then flags each item as Compliant, Not compliant or Needs-check. It's a self-assessment, not a legal determination.
