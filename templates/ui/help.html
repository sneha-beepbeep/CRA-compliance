What the statuses mean

Every status describes what has been entered here: an evidence item attached, a date passed, a filing time recorded. None of them is a determination about the product.

Art. 14 deadlines

  • Early warning: 24 elapsed hours from the awareness time.
  • Notification: 72 elapsed hours from the awareness time. Calendar hours. Weekends and holidays are not excluded.
  • Final report, actively exploited vulnerability: 14 days after a fix or mitigation is recorded as available (CRA Art. 14(2)(c)).
  • Final report, severe incident: one calendar month after the notification filing time. Calculated as one calendar month. Pending legal confirmation.

This tool does not draft or file reports. File with the single reporting platform yourself, then record the time you filed here.

The dashboard does not notify anyone. Check the clock strip yourself, including outside office hours.
